# Design Artifact Provenance

`../DESIGN.md` is the current visual system: **The Split Champion Board**. Keep the machine-readable
`.impeccable/design.json` sidecar aligned with its tokens and representative components.

`direction-options.json` and `sketches/*.png` are retained records of the early visual-direction
exploration. The Arcade ticket, split-flap, Miura-fold, and console concepts are alternatives, not
active interface direction. The sketches are historical references; a few predate the product's
rename from “Model” to “Body”. Do not treat their labels or layouts as current behavior. Preserve them
as provenance rather than rewriting them to match later implementation.
