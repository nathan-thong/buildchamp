## Affected compatibility exceptions
- aphelios: Exclude Aphelios from the initial pool because individual slots would be nonfunctional or would implicitly supply several other slots. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- jayce: Jayce is one base champion with two draft variants, so weighting and no-repeat identity are shared. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- hwei: Hwei remains one champion and is not split into form variants. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- elise: Elise is one base champion with two draft variants, so weighting and no-repeat identity are shared. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- nidalee: Nidalee is one base champion with Human and Cougar draft variants, so weighting and no-repeat identity are shared. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- gnar: Gnar is one base champion with Mini and Mega draft variants, so weighting and no-repeat identity are shared. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- shyvana: Shyvana remains one normal draft variant because R is an active, self-contained Dragon transformation rather than a permanent alternate kit. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- rek-sai: Rek’Sai remains one normal draft variant because W is an active state switch and Q/E contain their alternate casts inside their own slots; no slot is permanently replaced. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- kled: Kled is represented by one coherent Mounted variant rather than exposing a weaker, incomplete Dismounted roll. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- kayn: Kayn has two fixed draft variants under one base-champion identity: Rhaast and Shadow Assassin. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
- viego: Viego keeps one default-kit variant and R remains selectable as Heartbreaker. (reviewed for 16.17.1)
